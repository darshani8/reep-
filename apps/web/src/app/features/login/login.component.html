Who can sign in

Only people already on the programme roster — students, mentors and staff. There is no sign-up: your account exists before you first sign in, or it does not exist at all.

  • Use your college Google account, the one ending @{{ domain() }}.
  • A personal Gmail account will be refused unless that exact address is itself on the roster.
  • Not on the roster and think you should be? Ask the placement office — it cannot be fixed from this screen.

Sign-in is Google only. This page hands you to Google; the API checks the identity token Google signs — its signature, who it was issued for, and that the email is verified — matches the address against the roster, and hands back a signed session token held in an http-only cookie for twelve hours. Every page then verifies that token and the role for itself. A ?next= value is followed only when it is a path on this site.
