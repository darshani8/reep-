Colour

Both palettes shown at once — the left panel is always light, the right always dark, whatever theme you are reading this page in.

Status is monochrome here, and that is deliberate. Success, warning and error are brown steps (#8b5f1c, #62471f, #3a301f), not the usual green/amber/red. It holds the warm identity together, but it means colour alone cannot carry state — every status must also be named in words or marked by position. The Tone vocabulary below is what keeps that honest.

Typography

Translucency is a preference, not a requirement. Under prefers-reduced-transparency, or in a browser without backdrop-filter, .reep-glass falls back to the opaque --reep-glass-solid and .reep-ambient is removed entirely — an opaque surface beats unreadable text. In print, glass and neu both flatten to white with a hairline border.

Focus & fallbacks

One ring everywhere, and three graceful degradations that are part of the system, not afterthoughts.

*:focus-visible — 2px solid accent, 2px offset, z-index 1
Focused control

The z-index: 1 matters — without it the ring is clipped by the next table cell or sibling card wherever controls sit edge to edge.

degradations
ConditionWhat changes
prefers-reduced-transparency Glass becomes opaque --reep-glass-solid; blur removed; ambient bloom hidden.
@supports not backdrop-filter Same opaque fallback, so text never sits on an unblurred translucent ground.
@media print .no-print hidden; body white; glass and neu flattened to white with a 1px border and no shadow.
Skip link Off-screen at left: -9999px until focused, then pinned 8px from the top-left.

Token index

What to reach for, and when.

TokenUse it for
--reep-bg-defaultThe page ground. Never a card.
--reep-bg-paperAnything raised off the ground — cards, table wells, the app bar.
--reep-text-primaryBody copy and headings.
--reep-text-secondaryLabels, subtitles, column heads.
--reep-text-disabledHints and metadata — the third rank, not a disabled state.
--reep-secondary-mainThe one accent. Primary buttons, focus rings, links, active chips.
--reep-dividerEvery hairline. Sections separate with a rule, not a card edge.
--reep-action-hoverHover ground on text buttons and rows; also the banner fill.
--reep-action-selectedActive chip fill and selected nav item.
--reep-success / warning / error-mainStatus ink, via the Tone map — never a fill.
--reep-cell-wellInset data cells inside a table or grid.
--reep-bloom-a / b / cAmbient background blooms only. Decorative, and hidden under reduced transparency.

The tone vocabulary is the contract. Tone = 'good' | 'warning' | 'critical' | 'info' | 'neutral' | 'accent', mapped to ink in shared/kit/tone.ts. A screen picks a tone, never a colour — which is what lets the whole palette change in one file. In this theme the status .dark step equals .main, so one token serves both.
