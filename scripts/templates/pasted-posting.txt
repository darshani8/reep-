About the role

We are hiring a Business Analyst into our 2026 graduate intake. You will sit with
the Data & Insights team and turn messy operational data into decisions the
commercial team can act on.

What you will do
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI and Tableau
- Write SQL against our warehouse to answer questions from the business
- Model scenarios in advanced Excel, including pivot tables and Power Query
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders every fortnight

What we are looking for
- MBA or equivalent, graduating in 2026 or 2027
- Working knowledge of SQL and at least one BI tool
- Comfortable with statistics and A/B testing fundamentals
- Clear written and spoken communication in English

Nice to have
- Python for data analysis, pandas in particular
- Exposure to dbt or a similar transformation tool
