## Context

What forced a decision. The symptom, the constraint, the numbers you measured
(say how), and the files involved. Two or three paragraphs; a reader who knows
nothing about this should understand why "do nothing" was not an option.

## Decision

What we will do, in one paragraph a reviewer can quote. Name the files or
modules that carry it.

## Alternatives considered

- **The obvious one** — why it was rejected. Be specific: what it would have
  broken or cost.
- **Another** — why not.

## Consequences

- What gets easier.
- What gets harder, and what it costs — money, latency, a rule somebody must now
  remember.
- What enforces this decision (a test, a gate, a guard), or "nothing — review
  only", said plainly.
